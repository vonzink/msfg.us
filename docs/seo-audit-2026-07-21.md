# MSFG.us SEO Audit — 2026-07-21

**Scope:** Full site audit. Two surfaces audited because the cutover hasn't happened yet:

- **Live `msfg.us`** — the old GoHighLevel site (today's SEO baseline, Cloudflare → Google origin)
- **`staging.msfg.us`** — the new Next.js site in this repo (what cutover will ship)

Plus codebase review and keyword/competitor research. No SEO tool (Ahrefs/Semrush) is connected, so volume/difficulty are qualitative estimates; connect one via claude.ai connector settings for hard numbers.

---

## Executive Summary

The live msfg.us has close to **zero SEO equity**: the homepage `<title>` and meta description are both just "Mountain State Financial Group, LLC", state pages carry a copy-paste description ("Bismarck page"), there is no usable sitemap, unknown URLs soft-404 into `/thank-you`, and Google has indexed almost nothing (`site:msfg.us` surfaces roughly one page). That is bad news for today but good news for the cutover: **there is very little equity to lose and a greenfield to claim.**

The new Next.js site is dramatically better — unique titles/descriptions on all 25 sitemap URLs, exactly one H1 per page, an env-gated robots/noindex system, a CMS SEO editor (`/admin/seo`), and a real sitemap emitting canonical `https://msfg.us` URLs. The biggest risks are **cutover mechanics, not content**: the legacy-URL redirect map (cutover plan task 0.7) is unimplemented (every old GHL URL 404s on the new app), several money pages are missing canonicals, and title templates double the brand ("… | MSFG · MSFG").

**Top 3 priorities:**
1. **Replace `[PLACEHOLDER]` data before the site becomes indexable** — `/rates` shows placeholder rates with a stale timestamp, product pages show unverified "$1.4B+ / 4.9★ 612 reviews" stats, `/licensing` shows placeholder license numbers. On a YMYL site this is a compliance and trust problem before it's an SEO one.
2. **Ship the legacy 301 redirect map before cutover** — every old GHL URL 404s on the new app, including `msfg.us/tracyroberts`, which *currently ranks* for Bismarck mortgage searches.
3. **Fix the on-page mechanics batch** (double-brand titles, missing canonicals on the money pages, glossary missing from the sitemap), then **build local + informational content** — per-officer pages, state/office landers, DPA guides — the field where nationals can't follow and the regionals have nothing.

**Overall assessment:** old site = critical issues; new site = strong foundation, needs pre-cutover fixes + a content strategy.

VA IRRRL + FHA streamline), HELOC/cash-out, DSCR/investment, and HECM reverse.
